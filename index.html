    Build it
    with us.

    Join a team through try-outs or as a volunteer coach, or join the association as an associate in Finance, Media & Marketing, Operations or Partnerships. Recruitment is in English and open to every Esade student, whatever their nationality or language.

    1. STEP 1Open call

      Published on every channel, in English.

    2. STEP 2Apply

      CV, cover letter and a short video, through this website.

    3. STEP 3Shortlist

      Chosen by the Board: executive board and Heads.

    4. STEP 4Interview

      Formal, with set questions and a code of conduct.

    5. STEP 5Offer

      Offer and onboarding into your department.
